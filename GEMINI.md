# Yash Marketing Delivery & Payment App - Antigravity Project Guide

## Overview
- **Project Name**: Yash Marketing Delivery & Payment OS
- **Repository**: [yashmbarlota007/Delivery-Payment-App](https://github.com/yashmbarlota007/Delivery-Payment-App)
- **Default Branch**: `main`
- **Zero-Storage Online Workflow**: 100% cloud-managed via GitHub REST API & Google Apps Script GitHub Assistant
- **Purpose**: Dedicated Mobile & Web App for Field Delivery Staff & Payment Collection with Proof Photo upload.

## Two Core Tabs
1. **Delivery / Delivered Tab**: Live order queue for Out-for-Delivery orders, direct call/WhatsApp, and 1-tap "Mark as Delivered" (Stage 8 in Yash Marketing Backend).
2. **Payment Collection Tab**: Record Cash or Online (UPI/QR) payments with mandatory Payment Photo upload tagged to the exact Dealer & Order ID.
